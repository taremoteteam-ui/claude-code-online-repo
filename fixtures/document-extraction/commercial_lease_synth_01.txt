COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Commercial Lease Agreement (this "Lease") is made and entered into as of August 12, 2025, by and between Cascade Summit Holdings, LLC, an Ohio limited liability company ("Landlord"), and Brightleaf Analytics, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("Tenant"). Landlord and Tenant are sometimes referred to individually as a "Party" and collectively as the "Parties."

ARTICLE 1. PREMISES

Landlord hereby leases to Tenant, and Tenant hereby leases from Landlord, the premises located at 1420 Marketview Boulevard, Suite 300, Riverton, Ohio 44120 (the "Premises"), consisting of approximately 4,500 square feet of rentable area within the building commonly known as Marketview Commons.

Tenant shall have the non-exclusive right to use the common areas of the building in accordance with the rules and regulations established by Landlord.

ARTICLE 2. TERM

The initial term of this Lease shall be sixty (60) months, commencing on September 1, 2025 (the "Commencement Date") and expiring on August 31, 2030, unless sooner terminated in accordance with the terms of this Lease.

Provided that Tenant is not then in default, Tenant shall have one (1) option to renew the term for an additional sixty (60) months upon not less than one hundred eighty (180) days prior written notice to Landlord.

ARTICLE 3. RENT

Tenant shall pay to Landlord base rent in the amount of $12,750.00 per month (the "Base Rent"), due in advance on the first day of each calendar month, without demand, deduction, or offset.

In addition to Base Rent, Tenant shall pay its proportionate share of operating expenses, taxes, and insurance as additional rent.

ARTICLE 4. SECURITY DEPOSIT

Upon execution of this Lease, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord a security deposit in the amount of $25,500.00 as security for the faithful performance of Tenant's obligations under this Lease.

ARTICLE 5. USE

Tenant shall use the Premises solely for general office and software development purposes and for no other purpose without Landlord's prior written consent.

Tenant shall not use or permit the Premises to be used in any manner that violates any applicable law or the certificate of occupancy for the building.

ARTICLE 6. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIR

Tenant shall, at its own expense, keep the interior of the Premises in good order and repair. Landlord shall maintain the structural components and the common areas of the building.

ARTICLE 7. INSURANCE

Tenant shall maintain commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than One Million Dollars ($1,000,000.00) per occurrence and shall name Landlord as an additional insured.

ARTICLE 8. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublet any portion of the Premises without the prior written consent of Landlord, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld.

ARTICLE 9. GOVERNING LAW

This Lease shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Ohio.

ARTICLE 10. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This Lease constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties and supersedes all prior negotiations and agreements, whether written or oral.

PURCHASE AND SALE AGREEMENT

This Purchase and Sale Agreement (this "Agreement") is made and entered into as of October 2, 2025 (the "Effective Date"), by and between Emberline Capital Partners, LLC, a Nevada limited liability company ("Buyer"), and Foxglen Property Group, Inc., a Nevada corporation ("Seller").

ARTICLE 1. PROPERTY

Seller agrees to sell and convey to Buyer, and Buyer agrees to purchase from Seller, the real property and improvements located at 88 Old Cannery Road, Willow Bend, Nevada 89005 (the "Property"), together with all appurtenances and fixtures thereto.

ARTICLE 2. PURCHASE PRICE

The total purchase price for the Property shall be $1,250,000.00 (the "Purchase Price"), payable in immediately available funds at the Closing, subject to the prorations and adjustments set forth herein.

ARTICLE 3. EARNEST MONEY

Within three (3) business days after the Effective Date, Buyer shall deposit earnest money in the amount of $50,000.00 with the Escrow Agent, to be applied against the Purchase Price at the Closing or otherwise disbursed in accordance with this Agreement.

ARTICLE 4. CONTINGENCIES

Buyer's obligation to close is contingent upon the following: (a) Buyer shall have until October 22, 2025 (the "Due Diligence Deadline") to complete its inspections and investigations of the Property; and (b) Buyer obtaining financing on terms acceptable to Buyer in its sole discretion.

If Buyer is not satisfied with the results of its due diligence, Buyer may terminate this Agreement by written notice delivered on or before the Due Diligence Deadline, in which event the earnest money shall be refunded to Buyer.

ARTICLE 5. CLOSING

The closing of the transaction contemplated by this Agreement (the "Closing") shall occur on or before November 14, 2025, at the offices of the Escrow Agent or such other place as the parties may agree in writing.

At Closing, Seller shall deliver to Buyer a duly executed grant deed conveying good and marketable title to the Property, free and clear of all liens and encumbrances except the permitted exceptions.

ARTICLE 6. REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

Seller represents and warrants that Seller has full power and authority to convey the Property and that, to Seller's knowledge, there is no pending litigation affecting the Property.

ARTICLE 7. DEFAULT

If Buyer defaults, Seller's sole remedy shall be to retain the earnest money as liquidated damages. If Seller defaults, Buyer may seek specific performance or termination and a refund of the earnest money.

ARTICLE 8. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Nevada.

ARTICLE 9. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, and agreements, whether written or oral.

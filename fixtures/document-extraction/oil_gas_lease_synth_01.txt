OIL AND GAS LEASE

This Oil and Gas Lease (this "Lease") is made and entered into as of April 9, 2025, by and between Marguerite T. Halloran, a single person ("Lessor"), and Redstone Minerals Operating, LLC, a Texas limited liability company ("Lessee").

ARTICLE 1. LEASED PREMISES

Lessor, in consideration of the bonus paid and the covenants set forth herein, hereby grants, leases, and lets exclusively unto Lessee the following described land situated in Caldwell County, Texas, to-wit: Section 14, Township 5 South, Range 8 West, containing 160 gross acres, more or less (the "Leased Premises"), for the purpose of exploring for, developing, producing, and marketing oil and gas.

The interest covered by this Lease constitutes 160.00 net mineral acres.

ARTICLE 2. HABENDUM

This Lease shall remain in force for a primary term of three (3) years from the date hereof (the "Primary Term") and as long thereafter as oil or gas is produced from the Leased Premises in paying quantities.

ARTICLE 3. ROYALTY

Lessee shall deliver to Lessor, free of cost, as royalty a royalty of 3/16 of the gross proceeds received from the sale of oil and gas produced and saved from the Leased Premises.

ARTICLE 4. BONUS AND DELAY RENTALS

As consideration for the execution of this Lease, Lessee shall pay to Lessor a lease bonus of $500.00 per net mineral acre covered by this Lease.

If operations for drilling are not commenced on the Leased Premises on or before the first anniversary of the date hereof, this Lease shall terminate as to both parties unless Lessee pays or tenders a delay rental of $1,600.00 to Lessor.

ARTICLE 5. SHUT-IN ROYALTY

If a well capable of producing gas in paying quantities is located on the Leased Premises but is shut in, Lessee shall pay a shut-in royalty of $960.00 per well per year, and this Lease shall remain in force as though gas were being produced.

ARTICLE 6. PUGH CLAUSE

Notwithstanding anything to the contrary, upon expiration of the Primary Term this Lease shall terminate as to all lands not then included within a producing or spacing unit.

ARTICLE 7. SURFACE USE

Lessee shall bury pipelines below ordinary plow depth and shall pay for damages caused by its operations to growing crops and improvements on the Leased Premises.

ARTICLE 8. ASSIGNMENT

The rights of either party may be assigned in whole or in part, and the provisions of this Lease shall extend to the heirs, successors, and assigns of the parties.

ARTICLE 9. GOVERNING LAW

This Lease shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Texas.
